The top bar is not a top bar at all, it is a cloud.

Out of the cloud come lightning bolts: Calendar, Contacts, Tasks

When clicking on a lightning bolt, it rains and creates a beautiful pond with a beautiful ripple effect.

The ripples are rings for Calendar:
 1st ring (center) is today
 2nd ring is 3 days
 3rd ring is week
marked with colors for the different calendars


For Contacts, ripples are different "circles" of people
- people on lotus flowers
- other stuff floating

For Tasks, ripples are different priority levels
- gators for deadlines
